A GTA 6 meme in the form of an eviction notice: after 13 years on the console, GTA V is ordered to vacate within 30 days so GTA VI (keys: Jason & Lucia) can move in. The charges are itemised — Trevor's bail, an unsettled Shark Card tab, thrown controllers, takeaway boxes, a 214 GB download queue — totalling $1,115.95, stamped PAID. The make.fun ghost paid the bill in full with real cash it won in make.fun bounties. The notice was drawn from scratch in Python/PIL (`render-meme-v3.py`) as `meme-v3.png` (1080x1080), every line of it written for this meme.

The earlier memes are still in the repo: v2, the obituary poster (`meme-v2.png`), and v1, "Called in sick for GTA 6" (`meme.png`). The revised entry (v3) is the eviction notice, posted on X from @iwanhadi96 with the play code `makefun-sequin-dream-bracket` and a link to the bounty.
